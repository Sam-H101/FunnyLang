# the oil and the spark — a chef of fire, and the man with the lamp.
#
#   funny run extensive_examples/isekai/play.funny -- \
#       --origin chef --calling cleric --affinity fire --script scripts/the-oil-and-the-spark.txt
#
# A chef comes down already carrying, and using what they carry costs them no
# turn — so "pour, then light" is one turn for them and two for anybody else.
# That is this run's whole trick, and it is two rules that were always in the
# table being put together by a player rather than by an author.
#
# IT IS ALSO A RUN THAT GOES BADLY, and it is kept that way on purpose. The
# second floor is full of gas and there is a man standing in it holding a
# lamp. The obvious move takes the whole room. No rule in the table mentions
# him: rule 9 takes the room, and he is in the room.
#
# From there it compounds without anybody writing it down. The girl on the
# third floor has heard, and will not speak. The chapel goes up too, which is
# two more corruption for burning a sacred floor. By the fourth floor the
# corruption is past ten, the rooms arrive hollow, and it ends on the iron
# gallery with all three of them dead and the run with them.
#
# A chef who spent those same flasks on the rooms and not on the people gets
# further. That is the example's whole argument, and this is the script that
# makes it by failing rather than by succeeding.
#
# The kit: 1 Mend, 2 Ward, 3 Smite, 4 Consecrate, 5 Ember, 6 oil, 7 waterskin.
